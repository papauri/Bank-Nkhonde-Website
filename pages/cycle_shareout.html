How a Payout Is Worked Out

A member's payout is their interest refund plus their penalty share. The interest refund is the interest that member personally paid on their own loans. The penalty share is an equal slice of the group's penalty pool, split evenly across members, and only when this group's rules say penalties are shared out at cycle end. Contributed capital is not part of the payout, and a penalty is never refunded to the member who paid it.
